  FAQ's  

What are the check-in and check-out times?
The standard check-in time is 3:00 pm. Should you require an early check-in, we would recommend that the room is reserved a day in advance to guarantee early access. The check-out time is 12 noon. The late check-out can be confirmed a day in advance of your departure date and subject to room availability with additional charge as follows: Late check-out until 6:00 pm will be charged at 50% of the daily room rate (referring to the website rate) Late check-out after 6:00 pm will be charged one night.
Do you allow pets?
To reserve the overall atmosphere and avoid any dangers that might occur to both guests and pets, KokHan Hotel does not allow any pets in the resort.
Am I allowed to smoke in the hotel?
Being a wellness hotel, we encourage you to minimize smoking as for your own and other guests’ health benefits. Therefore, smoking indoors or in any of our public areas and facilities is not allowed. Should you wish to smoke, you may do so on the terrace or balcony of your own guest room. A fee equivalent to RM 1000 per incident will be charged to your account if a smoking incident occurs in your room during your stay.
Do you have free access wifi in your hotel?
Stay connected during your stay with 1GB bandwidth internet service provided throughout our guestrooms and public areas. Please log-in with your username and password provided at check-in. User name is your room no. Password is *followed by room no. eg: USER NAME: 2803 PASSWORD: *2803.
What is your linen & damage Policy?
Please note that additional charges are applicable for linen stains and other cleaning or damages caused intentionally or unintentionally. Penalties vary depending on the extent and severity of the damage. These include, but are not limited to, cleaning, repair, or replacement charges for stained walls, vomit, blood and food stain on linens, and broken furniture and fittings. The exact cost will be determined on a case-by-case basis by the Manager in charge.
