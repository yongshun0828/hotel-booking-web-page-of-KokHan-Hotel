Beach Volleyball

This is a fun and energetic team sport that can be played on the sand. Guests can form teams and compete against each other while enjoying the beautiful scenery.

Stand-up Paddleboarding

Stand-up paddleboarding is a relaxing activity that involves standing on a large board and paddling along the coast. The hotel can rent out boards to guests and provide lessons if needed.

Bonfire

Guests can gather around the fire, roast marshmallows, and enjoy the company of their fellow guests while listening to the sound of the waves.

Snorkel

Snorkeling is a popular activity that allows guests to explore the underwater world and observe marine life up close. The hotel can provide snorkeling gear and guides to lead guests to the best spots for snorkeling.

Surfing

The hotel offers a surfing activity where guests can learn to surf or improve their skills. The hotel provides surfboards and wetsuits, and instructors teach guests the basics of surfing in a safe and controlled environment.

Spa

After a long day of exploring the beach and mountain, guests may want to relax and unwind with a spa treatment. The hotel can offer a variety of spa services such as massages, facials, and body treatments.

Fitness Activity

For fitness enthusiasts, the hotel can offer yoga classes, beach runs, or mountain hikes with a personal trainer.

Bird Watching

The hotel offers guided bird watching tours for guests who want to observe local and migratory bird species. Guests meet with a knowledgeable guide early in the morning, who provides equipment like binoculars and bird guides.

Sunset Cocktails

The hotel's mixologist then creates a selection of delicious cocktails and mocktails using fresh fruits and premium spirits, all served with stunning views of the sunset.

Swimming

The swimming pool at the hotel is a recreational facility designed for guests to relax and enjoy. It is typically located outdoors or indoors and may be heated or unheated. The pool area is equipped with lounge chairs, umbrellas, and other amenities to create a comfortable and inviting atmosphere.

SUV

The hotel provides SUV services for guests who want to explore mountainous areas. These SUVs are designed to navigate rough terrain and offer a comfortable mode of transportation. Guests can book SUV services through the hotel and may be accompanied by a driver or guide.

Horseback Riding

Hotels offer horseback riding as an activity for guests. Riders of all levels can enjoy guided trail rides or lessons led by experienced guides/instructors for safety. Horseback riding is a unique way to explore the outdoors, but it may be offered seasonally or on specific days.
