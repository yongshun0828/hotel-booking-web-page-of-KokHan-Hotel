Standard Room

A standard hotel room already includes basic stuff. It includes a table, chairs, bed, dressing table, television, telephone, and a bathroom.

Deluxe City View Room

Rooms enjoy a classic décor, with a neutral colour palette, contemporary artwork and a selection of light brown and gold soft furnishings.

Deluxe Family Room

Travelling with family members or a group of friends? Our Deluxe Family Room is perfect for you and comfortably accommodates up to four adults or two adults and two children.

Luxury Room

The luxury room is tastefully decorated with high-quality materials, exquisite furnishings, and attention to detail. The color palette is often rich and inviting, combining warm tones with touches of gold or silver accents to create a regal ambiance. The room may feature plush carpets, elegant drapes, and ornate fixtures that add to its overall charm.

Deluxe Seaview

With sweeping sea views, this room is adorned with indigenous materials and nature-inspired tones evoking an airy feel, offering a refreshing welcome and comfortable stay.

Premier Sea

Rooms comes with a long balcony overlooking the white sandy beach and deep blue sea. Room size is 366 sq ft (approximately). Inter-connecting room with Deluxe Sea View room is available. Limited unit and subject to availability.
